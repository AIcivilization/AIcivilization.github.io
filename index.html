dsh-vps-manager

在 DSH 对话里管理你的 VPS，像 SSH 一样顺手。Run your VPS from a DSH conversation, as smoothly as over SSH.

  • 21 条零 token 命令21 zero-token commands看状态、装软件、重启等机器回来，不走模型Status, installs, reboot-and-wait — without the model
  • AI 操作，按风险分级确认AI actions, confirmed by risk只读自动、改动问你、高危再问；改防火墙前设好自动恢复Read-only runs, changes ask, risky ones ask twice; firewall edits get an auto-restore
  • 对话里的终端与文件管理Terminal and files in the conversation真终端，vim、top 都能用；拖拽上传，改前自动备份A real terminal (vim, top); drag-and-drop uploads with backups before edits
  • 29 条运维菜谱29 maintenance recipes可重复执行；AI 做成的事一句话存成菜谱Repeatable; save anything the AI did as a recipe in one sentence
